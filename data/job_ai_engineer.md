# AI Engineer — Nimbus Rentals (fictional)

Location: Bangkok (hybrid, 3 days in office)
Salary: 45,000 – 60,000 THB per month
Type: Full-time

## About the role
Nimbus Rentals runs a marketplace for renting equipment. You will build AI features that help
renters find the right equipment and help our support team answer questions faster.

## Requirements
- 2+ years of software development experience
- Python and FastAPI
- Experience with LLM APIs (Claude, OpenAI or Gemini)
- Retrieval-augmented generation and vector databases (pgvector, Pinecone or similar)
- Good written and spoken English

## Nice to have
- LangChain or LlamaIndex
- Docker and CI/CD
- Experience with marketplaces or booking systems

Thai language is not required.
